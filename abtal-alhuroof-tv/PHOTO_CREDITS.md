# Photos bundled in Abtal Academy TV — attribution & rights

The photos in this educational app are **photographs**, rather than images generated by AI. They were selected from Wikimedia Commons because their authors stated an open licence or public-domain dedication. The app is **not affiliated with or endorsed by** any of the YouTubers pictured. Names are used factually in a recognition/educational quiz. No external YouTube links or streams are opened by the app.

Photos are resized and padded onto a 380×260 dark background for **offline** Android TV compatibility. Their copyright licences remain with their original authors; where ShareAlike applies, the modified photograph is shared under that same licence. Source pages give the author's original credit and licence terms.
